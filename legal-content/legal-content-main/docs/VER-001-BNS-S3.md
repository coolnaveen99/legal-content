# VER-001 BNS section 3 body check

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, Act 45 of 2023, text as on 6 October 2025.

Official heading: General explanations.

Official subsection (5): "When a criminal act is done by several persons in furtherance of the common intention of all, each of such persons is liable for that act in the same manner as if it were done by him alone."

Local file `topics/bns/s-3.json` is titled "Joint liability and common intention" and discusses section 3(5). That sentence matches. The file does not cover subsections (1) to (4) or (6) to (9), which the same section also contains. Those subsections were read only far enough to confirm the section is broader than common intention.

Case names already in the local note, including Mahbub Shah and Barendra Kumar Ghosh, were not re-checked against the judgments. The topic stays `in-progress`.
