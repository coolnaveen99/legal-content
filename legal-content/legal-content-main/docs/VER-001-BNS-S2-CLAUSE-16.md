# VER-001 BNS section 2, clause 16 and later clauses

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, text as on 6 October 2025.

Clause (16) "Judge" means a person officially designated as a Judge, and includes a person empowered to give a definitive judgment in a civil or criminal proceeding, or a judgment that would be definitive if not appealed or if confirmed, and a member of a body empowered to give such a judgment. Illustration: a Magistrate who can sentence to fine or imprisonment on the charge is a Judge.

Also read:

- (28) public servant begins with every commissioned officer in the Army, Navy or Air Force, and every Judge, including a person empowered to discharge adjudicatory functions. The explanations read say these persons are public servants whether or not appointed by the Government, and a person in actual possession of the situation is a public servant despite a legal defect in title. The rest of the public-servant list was not extracted. A Municipal Commissioner is given as a public servant.
- (29) reason to believe requires sufficient cause to believe, but not otherwise.
- (30) special law means a law applicable to a particular subject.
- (31) valuable security means a document which is, or purports to be, a document creating, extending, transferring, restricting, extinguishing or releasing a legal right, or acknowledging a legal liability or the absence of a certain legal right.
- (33) voluntarily means causing an effect by means intended to cause it, or by means known or believed likely to cause it at the time.
- (34) will means any testamentary document.
- (35) woman means a female human being of any age.
- (36) wrongful gain means gain by unlawful means of property to which the person gaining is not legally entitled.
- (37) wrongful loss means loss by unlawful means of property to which the person losing it is legally entitled.
- (38) gaining wrongfully includes retaining as well as acquiring. Losing wrongfully includes being kept out of property as well as being deprived of it. The extract ended during this clause.
