# VER-001 BNS section 2 body check

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, Act 45 of 2023, text as on 6 October 2025.

Official heading: Definitions.

Clauses read and matching the local note in `topics/bns/s-2.json`:

- (1) "act" denotes a series of acts as well as a single act.
- (2) "animal" means any living creature other than a human being.
- (3) "child" means any person below the age of eighteen years.
- (5) "Court" means a Judge, or a body of Judges, empowered to act judicially, when acting judicially.
- (6) "death" means the death of a human being unless the context shows otherwise.
- (7) "dishonestly" means doing anything with the intention of causing wrongful gain to one person or wrongful loss to another.
- (8) "document" includes an electronic and digital record.
- (9) "fraudulently" means doing anything with the intention to defraud but not otherwise.
- (10) the pronoun "he" is used of any person, whether male, female or transgender. "Transgender" has the meaning in section 2(k) of the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019.

Clause (4) and the clauses after (10), including counterfeit, were not re-read. The topic stays `in-progress`.
