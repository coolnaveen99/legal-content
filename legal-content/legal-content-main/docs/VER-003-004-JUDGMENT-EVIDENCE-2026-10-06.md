The judgment distinguishes the summary remedy under Section 125 CrPC from the substantive maintenance right under Section 20(3) of the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956. An unmarried Hindu daughter who has attained majority may pursue the Section 20(3) right where the statutory conditions are met, but that right is not enlarged into the Magistrate's Section 125 CrPC jurisdiction.
