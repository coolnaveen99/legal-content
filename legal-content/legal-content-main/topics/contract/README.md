# Law of Contract (India)

Indian Contract Act, 1872 — canonical section-wise subject.

## Canonical structure

The statutory unit is **one provision = one canonical topic/page**. Contract must follow the same navigation model used by BNS, BNSS and BSA.

### Indian Contract Act — operative provisions

- Sections **1–75**: individual section topics, including s. 19A and all statutory provisions in the range.
- Sections **76–123**: **repealed** former Sale of Goods provisions; do not create current Contract study topics for these repealed sections.
- Sections **124–238**: individual section topics, including s. 178A.
- Each section has its own canonical topic ID and URL target.
- Grouped files such as `ica-s-26-30.json` and `ica-s-37-45.json` are supplementary/migration source material only; they are not the canonical statutory navigation unit.

## Subject boundaries

- Indian Contract Act material remains the core Contract subject.
- Sale of Goods Act, 1930 and Indian Partnership Act, 1932 are separate statutes and must not be treated as merged statutory sections of the Indian Contract Act.
- Specific Relief Act material is related subject matter, not an ICA section.

## Content gate

New section records created by the restructure are `source_check_required`. They must be independently expanded and verified before `VERIFIED`, and separately promoted to `PUBLISHED`.
