## Fresh judgment rule

New judgments may enter the active catalog only after:

1. authoritative issuing-court source identification;
2. case identity and citation verification;
3. proposition and holding verification against the source;
4. later-treatment/current-law review where applicable; and
5. repository validation.

Secondary or AI-generated material alone is insufficient for active publication.

## Archive

Archived records remain recoverable for future authoritative re-verification. They are excluded from the active judgment catalog until that verification is completed.
