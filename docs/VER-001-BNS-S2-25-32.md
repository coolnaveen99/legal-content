# VER-001 BNS section 2, clauses 25–27 and 32

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, text as on 6 October 2025.

- (25) "omission" denotes a series of omissions as well as a single omission.
- (26) "person" includes any company or association or body of persons, whether incorporated or not.
- (27) "public" includes any class of the public or any community.
- (32) "vessel" means anything made for the conveyance by water of human beings or of property.
- (39) words and expressions used but not defined in the Sanhita, but defined in the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, have the meanings assigned there.

Public-servant descriptions additionally read:

- (c) every officer of a Court, including a liquidator, receiver or commissioner, whose duty is to investigate or report, make, authenticate or keep a document, take charge or dispose of property, execute a judicial process, administer an oath, interpret, or preserve order, and every person specially authorised by a Court to perform any of those duties.
- (d) every assessor or member of a panchayat assisting a Court or public servant.
- (g) every Government officer whose duty is to prevent offences, give information of offences, bring offenders to justice, or protect public health, safety or convenience.
- (h) every officer whose duty is to take, receive, keep or expend property on behalf of the Government, or to survey, assess, contract, execute a revenue-process, investigate or report on its pecuniary interests, keep related documents, or prevent infraction of a law protecting those interests.
