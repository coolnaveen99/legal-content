# Student Content Enhancement Specification

## Purpose

Enhance the preserved `codepackr-law` baseline for law students without replacing or shortening the migrated material.

## Required enhancement direction

Each enhanced topic should progressively cover:

1. Learning objectives
2. Definition and core concept
3. Legal principle/doctrine
4. Statutory framework
5. Essential ingredients/elements
6. Detailed explanation
7. Practical examples and illustrations
8. Distinctions from closely related concepts
9. Relevant, verified case law and ratio
10. Problem-question/application analysis
11. Exam-answer structures for short, 10-mark and 16-mark questions
12. Key takeaways/revision points
13. Authoritative sources
14. Current-law verification

## Preservation rule

The existing migrated topic remains the baseline. Enhancement is additive.

Do not:
- delete or overwrite the legacy substantive material;
- replace detailed legacy material with generated summaries;
- fabricate authorities or citations;
- mark an enhanced topic published without verification;
- introduce AI-generated legal propositions as authoritative without verification.

## Status

New enhancement content should begin as `planned` or `in-progress`. It may become `verified` only after source and current-law checks. Publication is a separate decision.

## Exam focus

The enhancement layer is designed to help a student construct a proper answer, including a 10-mark or 16-mark response. It is not a rigid answer template: the structure must follow the actual legal issue.

## AI sequencing

AI provider implementation remains the final product phase. All deterministic schema, source, legal verification, content enrichment, exam structure, UX and publication-readiness work must precede it.
