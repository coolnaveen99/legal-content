## Scope

Sections 3–9 cover:
- communication of proposals, acceptance and revocation;
- completion of communication;
- revocation of proposals and acceptances;
- modes of revocation;
- absolute and unqualified acceptance;
- acceptance by performance; and
- express and implied promises.

## Statutory verification

| Section | Verification result |
|---|---|
| 3 | Communication is constituted by an act or omission intended to communicate, or having the effect of communicating, the proposal, acceptance or revocation. |
| 4 | Completion rules distinguish proposal, acceptance and revocation, including the postal/transmission rule for acceptance. |
| 5 | Revocation timing is tied to completion of communication of acceptance against the proposer/acceptor. |
| 6 | Revocation is recognised through notice, lapse of time, failure of condition precedent, and qualifying death/insanity of the proposer. |
| 7 | Acceptance must be absolute and unqualified and must follow the prescribed or usual/reasonable mode. |
| 8 | Performance of proposal conditions or acceptance of offered consideration operates as acceptance in the statutory circumstances. |
| 9 | Promises are express when made in words and implied when made otherwise than in words. |
