## Repealed range

ICA ss. 76–123 are repealed. The former Sale of Goods material is governed by the Sale of Goods Act, 1930 and must not be represented as operative ICA sections.

## Allied statutes

The Sale of Goods Act, 1930 and Indian Partnership Act, 1932 are separate statutes. They must not be merged into an ICA section topic. Their own section-wise catalogues should be handled as separate statute/subject work.
