## Source checked

The official India Code text of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 identifies section 120 as “Presumption as to absence of consent in certain prosecution for rape”. It applies to a prosecution for rape under BNS section 64(2), where sexual intercourse by the accused is proved and the woman states in evidence before the Court that she did not consent. Official source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/20063/1/aa202347.pdf. The NCRB Compendium reproduces the same provision: https://cytrain.ncrb.gov.in/staticpage/web_pages/ChaptersBSA.html.

## Changes made

- Replaced incorrect references to “section 115 BSA” with “section 120 BSA” in the three affected BNS topic files.
- Clarified in section 63/64 material that section 120's presumption is limited to the statutory prerequisites for a prosecution under BNS section 64(2); it is not a blanket presumption for every rape prosecution.
- Corrected section 70 commentary that had claimed section 120 automatically creates a presumption against all accused in every gang-rape prosecution. It now warns not to extend the presumption to a section 70 charge without establishing that the statutory scope applies.
- Replaced an overbroad section 63 commentary claim that “any vulvar penetration or contact with labia majora satisfies the actus reus” with narrower wording that tracks Explanation 1 without deciding that every contact satisfies all offence elements.
- Corrected the section 63 heading that still labelled the provision “BSA s. 115”.
