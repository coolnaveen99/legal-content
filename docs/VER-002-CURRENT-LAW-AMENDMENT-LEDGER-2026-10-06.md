## What this resolves

1. Transition-sensitive source metadata for Industrial Relations, Industrial Disputes and Income-tax has been corrected/annotated.
2. Historical IPC/CrPC treatment remains explicitly separated from current criminal-law material.
3. The repository now has a durable VER-002 evidence record instead of relying on implicit assumptions.

## What remains

A complete VER-002 closure still requires proposition-level review of each in-scope topic that cites an affected statute, including amendment/commencement/savings analysis where material. Source-level currency alone is insufficient.
