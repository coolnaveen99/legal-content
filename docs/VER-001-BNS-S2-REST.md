# VER-001 BNS section 2, further clauses

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, text as on 6 October 2025.

Clause (4) "counterfeit": a person counterfeits who causes one thing to resemble another, intending by that resemblance to practise deception, or knowing deception is likely. Explanation 1: the imitation need not be exact. Explanation 2: if the resemblance might deceive, intention or knowledge is presumed until the contrary is proved. The local note already records these two explanations.

Also read:

- (11) nothing is in good faith if done or believed without due care and attention.
- (12) Government means the Central Government or a State Government.
- (13) harbour includes shelter, food, drink, money, clothes, arms, ammunition, conveyance, or other help to evade apprehension.
- (14) injury means any harm illegally caused to body, mind, reputation or property.
- (15) illegal covers an offence, a prohibition by law, or a ground for a civil action. A person is legally bound to do what it is illegal to omit.
- (17) life means the life of a human being unless the context shows otherwise.
- (18) local law means a law applicable only to a particular part of India.
- (19) man means a male human being of any age.
- (20) month and year are reckoned by the Gregorian calendar.
- (21) movable property includes property of every description except land and things attached to the earth, or permanently fastened to anything attached to the earth.
- (22) singular includes plural, and plural includes singular, unless the context shows otherwise.
- (23) oath includes a solemn affirmation and a declaration required or authorised by law.
- (24) offence generally means a thing made punishable by the Sanhita, with the stated extensions to special or local law in the listed chapters and sections, and the six-month threshold in the second extension.
