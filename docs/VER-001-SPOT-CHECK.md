# VER-001 spot check — BNS section 1

Source: India Code bitstream `123456789/20062`, Act 45 of 2023, text as on 6 October 2025.

India Code heading: "1. Short title, commencement and application."

India Code subsections read in this pass:

- (1) The Act may be called the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
- (2) Commencement is by Central Government notification, and different dates may be appointed for different provisions.
- (3) Every person is liable for an act or omission contrary to the Sanhita of which he is guilty within India.
- (4) A person liable under a law in force in India to be tried for an offence committed beyond India is dealt with under the Sanhita as if the act had been committed within India.

The first four points match the text read. Subsections (5) and (6) were not re-read, so the topic stays `in-progress`.
