# Legal Content — Content Depth Standard — 2026-10-08

## Purpose

This standard applies to every legal subject and every canonical topic in coolnaveen99/legal-content.

## No artificial content-length limit

There is no minimum or maximum line, word, character, paragraph, or byte count for legal content.

Content length must be determined by the legal material required to teach and apply the topic correctly.

- A simple provision may appropriately be concise.
- A complex doctrine may require hundreds or thousands of lines.
- A major subject or highly interconnected topic may require very extensive treatment.
- Very large content is acceptable when it is legally relevant, accurate, structured, source-conscious, and useful.
- Content must never be padded merely to increase length.

A short answer is not automatically low quality, and a long answer is not automatically high quality. Depth is measured by substantive coverage.

## Required substantive-depth test

For each topic, cover all elements that are genuinely applicable, including as appropriate:

1. Exact governing statutory, constitutional or rule text and scope.
2. Legislative purpose and doctrinal foundation.
3. Definitions and important terms.
4. Every material ingredient or condition.
5. Provisos, explanations, exceptions and qualifications.
6. Detailed doctrinal analysis.
7. Relationship with connected provisions and doctrines.
8. Topic-specific examples and statutory illustrations.
9. Multiple fact patterns and problem applications where useful.
10. Meaningful distinctions from neighbouring concepts.
11. Material defences, limitations or counterarguments where applicable.
12. Remedies, procedure, forum and evidence only where genuinely relevant.
13. Material authoritative case law, with verified proposition or ratio where used.
14. Current-law, amendment, repeal and transition issues where applicable.
15. Short-answer, 10-mark and comprehensive or exam treatment where relevant.
16. Revision points and key takeaways.
17. Authoritative sources and verification metadata.

The list is a coverage checklist, not a template to fill mechanically. Omit genuinely inapplicable elements and expand materially important ones.

## Complexity-driven depth

Workers must scale depth to the topic:

- Foundational or simple topic: enough explanation to make the rule complete and usable.
- Moderate topic: full rule, elements, exceptions, examples, applications, distinctions and relevant authority.
- Complex or high-value topic: extensive doctrinal treatment, competing interpretations, material authorities, factual applications, procedural or remedial implications and exam/application treatment.
- Major doctrine or subject: build a treatise-level treatment where the law warrants it.

Do not truncate a topic merely because another topic is shorter.

## Quality labels

substantive-topic-specific-v1 means the topic contains genuine topic-specific substantive treatment. It does not mean a fixed word count, a guaranteed exhaustive treatise, or completion of current-law or case-law verification.

COMPLETE_LOCKED means the subject has completed the applicable scaffold or enhancement remediation gate described by its audit. It does not by itself mean that all legal verification, case-law verification or production publication gates are closed.

## Insufficient-content trigger

If a topic is materially too short, generic, boilerplate-driven, repetitive, or missing necessary substantive coverage, that is a valid quality finding even when its enhancement object exists or a subject has an enhancement lock.

Such a finding must be recorded as a documented quality trigger before reopening a locked subject; do not silently redo locked work.

## Preservation rule

Existing substantive material must be preserved and improved unless there is an evidence-backed reason to remove or correct it. Never replace deep material with a short generic summary.

## Publication rule

A topic must not be promoted to verified or published merely because it has the expected JSON fields or passes schema validation. Substantive depth, legal verification and publication are separate gates.
