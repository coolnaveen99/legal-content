## Per-topic definition of enhancement complete

A topic is enhancement-complete only when:
1. it is no longer scaffold-level;
2. its content is topic-specific;
3. examples test the actual rule;
4. application reasoning is present where applicable;
5. distinctions are accurate where applicable;
6. 10/16-mark structures are topic-specific where applicable;
7. relationships are meaningful where applicable;
8. current-law warnings are explicit where relevant;
9. authoritative source metadata is present;
10. preservation passes.

This does **not** make the topic legally verified or published. Those remain separate gates.
