## Exact scope checked

The three subsections in the existing topic were compared with the text of section 106 reproduced in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs' official Companies Act, 2013 PDF, printed page 76:

- **s.106(1):** articles may restrict voting in respect of shares registered in the member's name where calls or other sums presently payable remain unpaid, or the company has exercised a right of lien.
- **s.106(2):** the company cannot prohibit voting on a ground other than the grounds in subsection (1).
- **s.106(3):** on a poll, a person entitled to multiple votes need not use all votes or cast every vote used in the same way; the statutory text also addresses a proxy where allowed and another person entitled to vote for the member.
